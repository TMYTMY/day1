# Business glossary

Use these terms when someone asks about revenue or products.

## Booked revenue

Booked revenue means the `revenue_usd` column on the `revenue` table.
Do not invent another amount column.

## Products

- Atlas — long-running product
- Harbor — long-running product
- Northstar — flagship product; launched later than Atlas and Harbor

## Regions

Revenue is recorded by `region`. Common values include APAC, EMEA, and NA.

## Daily active users

DAU means the `dau` column on the `dau` table, broken down by `platform`
(ios, android, web) and `country`.
