Activation is the first day a user is counted in daily active users for a platform. Daily active users are the dau column on the dau table. The grain is event date, platform, and country. Platforms are ios, android, and web. Countries in this extract are US, GB, DE, HK, and SG, and they partition a platform, so summing countries gives the platform total. Summing platforms can count one person twice.

New users are the new_users column, not a second table. A launch week raises both columns and is not, by itself, a change in definition. Growth owns this glossary. Finance may read it when a revenue question mentions a launch, but finance does not redefine activation. Do not call the revenue_usd column a user count. Campaign names live outside the warehouse. If a question says active users or new users, start here before opening the revenue table.
