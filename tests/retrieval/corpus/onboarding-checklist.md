First day checklist for a new analyst. Read the shared naming note, then the glossary for your own role. Finance starts with booked revenue. Growth starts with activation and daily active users. Confirm you can see only the tables your role is allowed to query. Finance queries revenue. Growth queries dau. Do not borrow a login to see the other table.

Next, read the known data issues note and the refresh runbook. Know which defects are already planted in the demo extract so you do not treat them as a fresh incident. Then pick one metric and answer one narrow question, with the grain written down. Products are Atlas, Northstar, and Harbor. Do not start with a year-long extract. Ask a teammate to read the sentence before you paste it into a deck. This checklist is shared and does not replace the role glossary. If a term is missing, add it to the glossary rather than to this list.
