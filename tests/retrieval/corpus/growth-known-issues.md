Growth's known issues are about the dau table only. Summing daily active users across ios, android, and web counts a person on two devices twice, and the extract has no identity key to undo that. Countries partition a platform, so the safe total is the sum of countries inside one platform. A launch week lifts the level of both dau and new_users. That lift is in the data on purpose and is not a duplicate row.

Nulls are not expected in platform or country. If one appears, stop and check the refresh runbook rather than filling it with web or US. Revenue defects such as a null channel, a euro amount, or a late Harbor backfill are listed on the finance note and the shared known-issues note. Do not copy those rows into a growth chart to explain a dau dip. The two tables share a calendar and nothing else. Write the limitation next to any number that adds platforms together.
