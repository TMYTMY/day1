Booked revenue is the revenue_usd column on the revenue table. Finance records it when an order is accepted, before refunds and before the month-end adjustment. Recognized revenue is that same column after the close removes cancelled orders. The products in this extract are Atlas, Northstar, and Harbor. Regions are AMER, EMEA, and APAC. Channels are direct, partner, and self-serve.

A new finance analyst should not add a second revenue column and should not treat a campaign name as a product. Finance owns this definition. Growth may read the column when joining campaign activity, but growth does not change the definition. Refunds stay in the finance close workbook and are not a separate warehouse table. If a question says sales or bookings, it still means this column unless a newer metric file says otherwise. Check this glossary before writing a number into a deck. The grain of a revenue row is event date, product, region, and channel. Summing two channels double counts an order only when the source system split it, which the daily load does not do on purpose.
