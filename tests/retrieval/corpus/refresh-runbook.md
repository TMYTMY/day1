The warehouse refresh is a nightly batch. It lands the revenue table and the dau table for the latest closed day, then writes a short log line. A new analyst checks the log before quoting either table. If the log is missing, do not fill the gap by hand. Wait for the next run or ask the person who owns the batch.

The refresh does not change metric definitions. Booked revenue stays the revenue_usd column. Daily active users stay the dau column. Products, regions, channels, platforms, and countries keep the names in the naming note. A backfill is a second batch for rows that missed the daily load, and it is labelled as a backfill rather than rewritten over history in silence. Harbor is the product that has needed this most often. After a backfill, rerun the monthly trend before sending it. The runbook is shared. Finance and growth both read it, and neither team edits the other team's glossary from here.
