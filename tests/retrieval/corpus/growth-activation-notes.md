Before a campaign, growth checks three things in the dau table. First, the previous seven days of daily active users by platform should be present for ios, android, and web. Second, new users should move in the same direction as daily active users unless the campaign is a win-back, which this extract cannot see. Third, the country mix should still be US, GB, DE, HK, and SG. A missing country is a load gap, not a real exit.

Do not read booked revenue as a substitute for activation. The revenue table can confirm that Northstar, Atlas, or Harbor had orders in the same week, and that is only context. The campaign brief should quote dau and new_users, with the platform named. Partner and direct are revenue channels, not user platforms. If the nightly refresh has not landed, wait for the runbook rather than copying yesterday's chart into the brief. Growth signs this check. Finance does not.
