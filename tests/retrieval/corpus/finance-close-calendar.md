The finance close starts on the first business day after the month ends and aims to finish within five business days. Day one is a tie-out of booked revenue to the order system. Day two is the refund reserve. Day three is the channel check for partner and direct. Day four is the region check for AMER, EMEA, and APAC. Day five is the sign-off note that the revenue table is safe to quote.

Do not quote a month that is still inside this calendar. Harbor, Atlas, and Northstar all follow the same close. There is no separate close for daily active users; that table is owned by growth and is only a cross-check when a revenue question mentions a launch week. The close workbook is not in the warehouse. If a column is missing, ask finance operations, do not invent a substitute metric. A late file does not move the calendar. It is noted in the known issues list and the month stays open until the sign-off sentence is written.
